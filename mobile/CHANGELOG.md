### LX Music+ 1.0.0

First release of LX Music+, an unofficial fork of [LX Music](https://github.com/lyswhut/lx-music-mobile) by lyswhut.

- New home page, library, stats, charts from many platforms, radio and daily mix
- More sources: YouTube, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, KHInsider; combined search, artists and albums
- No source bundled: a Sources page with a store (Install all), Music APIs and source addons from a link or file,
  and a prompt that leads there when nothing installed can play a song
- Import from other platforms (Spotify, Deezer, Apple Music, YouTube, SoundCloud, Last.fm, NetEase, CSV)
- Last.fm recommendations and scrobbling, Import & Sync with simple device pairing
