### LX Music+ 1.2.0

- Collected artists and albums now sync between devices (both devices need this version)
- Song radio: right-click a song (desktop) or open its menu (Android) and pick "Song radio"
- Radio learns from skips: skipped songs and similar music come up less for a while, and two skips of the same kind
  drop those songs from the queue; one artist no longer takes over the radio
- Dynamic background keeps the colours of the cover: dark mode only darkens them, light mode can be colourful
- Upcoming lyrics stay readable over light covers in light mode
- After an update the app shows what's new instead of offering the update again
- When Android first asks to allow installing apps, the update continues by itself once it's allowed

### LX Music+ 1.1.0

- Radio from an artist, an album or a playlist: its songs mixed with similar songs and artists, without end
- The home Radio now mixes some songs you know (mostly ones you played or loved recently) in with the new ones
- "Artists for you" on the home page changes every time the page loads or is refreshed
- Dynamic background: on by default in the player and the play bar, it follows the light / dark theme, and the text
  and the buttons stay readable over any cover
- No grey flash between songs: the cover of the song before stays until the cover of the new one has loaded
- Playlists: the sorts a category can't use are hidden (KuWo, QQ Music)
- Swipe the song title in the mini player left or right for the next or the previous song
- Sync reconnects by itself when its connection dropped while the app was in the background
- The dynamic background no longer turns grey after the app was in the background

### LX Music+ 1.0.0

First release of LX Music+, an unofficial fork of [LX Music](https://github.com/lyswhut/lx-music-mobile) by lyswhut.

- New home page, library, stats, charts from many platforms, radio and daily mix
- More sources: YouTube, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, KHInsider; combined search, artists and albums
- No source bundled: a Sources page with a store (Install all), Music APIs and source addons from a link or file,
  and a prompt that leads there when nothing installed can play a song
- Import from other platforms (Spotify, Deezer, Apple Music, YouTube, SoundCloud, Last.fm, NetEase, CSV)
- Last.fm recommendations and scrobbling, Import & Sync with simple device pairing
